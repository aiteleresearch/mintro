Legal

Risk disclosure.

Mining small coins is a bet, not an income. Read this before you start.

Last updated 6 November 2026 · Aitele Research LLP

  1. 01Not advice

    Not financial advice

    Nothing on this site or in the app is financial, investment or tax advice. A coin's score, a listing, or an exchange shown next to a coin is not a recommendation to mine, buy, hold or sell it.

  2. 02Estimates

    “Mined” is an estimate

    The amounts MINTRO shows as mined are estimates from your measured speed. What counts is what the pool pays out, and a pool pays only once your balance passes its minimum payout. Your total earnings are set by your computer's speed; spreading it across a basket spreads the bet, it doesn't multiply it.

  3. 03Value

    Coins can be worth nothing

    Many coins in the basket are early-stage, and some have no market at all; they may never be worth anything. Coins that do trade can be thinly traded and very volatile, and can lose most of their value quickly.

  4. 04Electricity

    Electricity and hardware

    Mining keeps your processor (and, for some coins, your graphics card) busy. That uses electricity, makes heat and fan noise, and adds wear. The electricity can cost more than what you mine. MINTRO's Auto mode pauses on battery and steps back when you're using the PC, but mining still loads the machine.

  5. 05Third parties

    Pools, exchanges and wallets

    Pools can go offline, change their payout rules or stop supporting a coin. Exchanges can delist coins or fail. MINTRO doesn't control any of them, and coins held on a pool or an exchange are only as safe as that service.

  6. 06Antivirus

    Antivirus warnings

    Antivirus software often flags mining engines, because mining software is also abused by malware. MINTRO explains a block on the card when it happens, and the antivirus guide says how to check the files. Download MINTRO only from the official link on this site.

  7. 07Wallets

    Keep your wallets safe

    Whoever holds a wallet's files or backup can spend its coins. Keep a copy of MINTRO's data folder somewhere safe and private; if it's lost and you have no other backup, the coins in those wallets are gone.

  8. 08Law & tax

    Law and tax

    Rules on mining and crypto assets, and on taxing them, differ from country to country and change. Following them where you live is your responsibility.
