The basics

What is MINTRO?

MINTRO is a free, open-source crypto mining app for Windows 10 and 11. It floats on your desktop as a small card, mines a basket of early-stage coins that suit an ordinary processor, tunes itself to your PC, and pays every share straight to your own wallets.

Is MINTRO really free? What's the catch?

It's free, with every feature included, and MINTRO takes no cut of what you mine. It's paid for by coin projects that list their coins in MINTRO's catalog. Listed coins are always labelled and scored like every other coin, and you can switch off featured coins' guaranteed share in Settings.

Is MINTRO open source?

Yes, under the GPL-3.0 licence. The app, the website and the release tools are on GitHub: read the code, build it yourself, or contribute.

What do I need to run it?

A 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC. WebView2, which MINTRO's window uses, is built into Windows 11 and up-to-date Windows 10. RandomX coins need about 2.5 GB of free memory for full speed. Download and install details.

Does MINTRO work on a Mac, Linux, Android or iPhone?

No. MINTRO is a Windows app. On a phone, the website offers to send you the link so you can open it on your PC.

Can MINTRO mine Bitcoin?

No. Bitcoin is mined by ASICs, machines built for nothing else, and a laptop would earn practically nothing. MINTRO mines coins whose algorithms run well on ordinary processors, such as RandomX and GhostRider.

Which coins does MINTRO mine?

CPU coins on the rplant pool that its engines can mine: Monero, Zephyr, Salvium, Raptoreum and newer coins with no market yet, plus coins listed by their projects. See the full list of coins.

Do I need a graphics card?

No. MINTRO mines with your processor. If your PC has a discrete NVIDIA, AMD or Intel Arc graphics card, it can also mine GPU coins on a second lane: on by default on desktops, and your choice on laptops because of the heat.

Earnings

How much can my laptop earn?

Cents a day. A typical laptop mines about 0.00017 Monero a day, a few US cents, before electricity. On young coins with few miners the same laptop mines a much bigger share, but most of those coins never gain value. The guide shows the arithmetic.

Is mining on a laptop worth the electricity?

On a big coin such as Monero a laptop roughly breaks even with average electricity prices. The upside is holding a slice of early coins in case one takes off: a lottery ticket, not an income. MINTRO shows earnings after electricity so you can judge for yourself.

Does mining more coins at once earn more?

No. Your laptop has a fixed speed, and the basket spreads it across several bets. It changes what you hold, not how much compute you have.

When do I get paid?

The pool sends a coin to your wallet once you pass its minimum payout. MINTRO only includes a coin if your PC will reach that within about a week, so your coins don't sit stranded on the pool.

Does MINTRO take a cut?

No. Every share goes to your own wallet address. The mining engines keep their own small developer fees (XMRig 1%, SRBMiner-Multi about 0.85%, cpuminer-opt none), listed in the app's settings.

Where can I sell what I mine?

Each coin in MINTRO lists the exchanges the pool knows it trades on, and hardware wallets that can hold it. Some early coins have no market at all yet.

Do I have to pay tax on mined coins?

In many countries mined coins count as income at their value when you receive them. Rules differ, so check yours. MINTRO's tax report exports everything mined per day and coin, with its dollar value at the time, as a CSV file.

Your PC

Will MINTRO slow my laptop down?

In Auto mode it drops to idle priority the moment you use the PC and uses fewer threads while you work, then runs fully when the PC is idle. It pauses on battery. Like any sustained load it runs the fans; Eco mode keeps things cool and quiet.

Can mining damage my laptop?

Mining keeps the processor warm for hours, and heat is what wears a laptop over time. Processors slow themselves down before they overheat. Keep the vents clear, don't mine on battery, and use Eco if it runs hot. MINTRO's Auto mode backs off while you work and pauses on battery.

Does MINTRO mine on battery?

No. By default it pauses when your laptop is unplugged and carries on when you plug it back in. You can change that in Settings.

How much memory does MINTRO need?

RandomX coins need about 2.5 GB of free RAM for full speed. With less, MINTRO switches them to RandomX's 256 MB light mode so your PC never swaps. Other algorithms need far less.

What are Auto, Eco, Balanced and Max?

Power modes. Auto, the default, is tuned to your PC and adapts to what you're doing. Eco uses half the threads at the lowest priority. Balanced gives the best speed per watt. Max runs at full speed at normal priority.

Safety and privacy

Why does Windows Security flag it?

Every antivirus flags crypto-mining engines, including the open-source ones MINTRO uses, because criminals hide them on other people's PCs. MINTRO downloads them from their official releases and checks their fingerprints. When Windows Security blocks one, the card explains why and offers to exclude only MINTRO's miner folder, behind Windows' own admin prompt.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is the installer safe?

SmartScreen shows that screen for apps it hasn't yet seen many people install, and MINTRO is new and not code-signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The installer needs no admin rights, only writes to your own user folder, and Settings › Apps removes it.

What does MINTRO send over the internet?

It talks to the mining pool, to GitHub for engines and updates, to price sites, and to a coin's official wallet download when you create a wallet. It can count your PC in the public miner counter with a random ID, nothing else: no wallet, no name, no hardware. Settings has the off switch. Privacy details.

Where are my wallets, and what if I lose the seed words?

Wallets MINTRO makes live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MINTRO\wallets, each with a backup file holding its seed words. Write the seed words on paper: anyone with them can spend the coins, and without them a lost wallet can't be recovered.

How do I uninstall MINTRO?

Settings › Apps › MINTRO › Uninstall. Your settings and wallets stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MINTRO so you keep your coins; delete that folder after backing up the seed words.

Wallets and coin projects

Do I need a crypto wallet?

Yes, one address per coin, because the pool pays straight to you and MINTRO never holds your coins. For Monero, Zephyr, Salvium and MYT, MINTRO creates the wallet in one click with the coin's official tools; for others, each coin links to its official wallet.

How do I get my coin listed in MINTRO?

Coin projects apply with the form in the For coin projects section. A listed coin reaches every MINTRO PC with no setup on the miner's side, carries a label, and is scored like every other coin.
