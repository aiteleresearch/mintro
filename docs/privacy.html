Privacy.

What this website and the MINTRO app collect, what they connect to, and what stays on your PC.

Last updated 9 November 2026 · Aitele Research LLP

  1. 01In short

    The short version

    This website sets no cookies and runs no third-party analytics. It counts downloads and visitors anonymously, as described below, and receives personal data only when you send us a form. The MINTRO app keeps your wallets and settings on your PC, and MINTRO 0.4.1 sends nothing about you to Aitele Research.

  2. 02This website

    What the website uses

    Browsing collects nothing for us. Like any website, the server that delivers these pages sees your IP address in order to send them. To draw the page, your browser also loads fonts from Google Fonts, the 3D scene's library from jsDelivr, and the download count from GitHub's public API; those services receive your IP address under their own privacy policies. The download count is kept in your browser's session storage for a few minutes so it isn't fetched again on every visit.

    Counting downloads and visitors. The site shows how many times MINTRO has been downloaded and how many different browsers have visited. To count visitors without cookies, your browser keeps two small notes in its local storage — that it has been here before, and the date of its last visit — and each page view sends the server only those two yes/no answers, which it adds to daily totals. No ID, IP address or browsing history is stored. A download is counted when it starts; to count a repeat click once, the server remembers your IP address for ten minutes, in memory only.

  3. 03Forms

    The contact and listing forms

    The contact form sends your name, email address, topic and message; the listing form sends your project's details and a contact email. Either way it reaches the Aitele Research team as an email, and we send a confirmation to the address you gave. Emails are delivered through Resend, our email provider. We use what you send only to answer you (and, for a listing, to review your coin), and we never add you to a mailing list.

    To stop the forms being abused, the server remembers your IP address for a few minutes to limit how many messages it accepts from one connection; it isn't stored or logged. If the form can't reach us, your email app opens instead, with your message filled in.

  4. 04On your PC

    What the app keeps on your PC

    MINTRO keeps its settings, the wallets it creates (wallet files and a plain-text backup), the mining engines and a random install ID made on first run in its data folder, %LOCALAPPDATA%\MINTRO. None of it is uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app keeps that folder, so your wallets survive.

  5. 05Connections

    What the app connects to

    • Mining pools — your wallet address and your IP address, which mining needs; the pool uses the address to pay you.
    • MINTRO's website (mintro.aiteleresearch.com) — to check for updates and service notices every 6 hours, and to download a signed update when there is one. The check sends nothing about you or your PC beyond what any web request does, and isn't logged.
    • GitHub — as a fallback for update checks, to fetch the coin listings, and to download the mining engines from their projects' official releases.
    • Coin projects' official download sites — for the wallet tools used to create wallets (for example, getmonero.org).
    • WhatToMine and CoinGecko — public price and difficulty data for the estimates.

    Each of these services handles what it receives under its own privacy policy.

  6. 06Miner counter

    The public miner counter

    MINTRO can count itself in a public tally of active miners. When that counter is switched on, the app sends, every 10 minutes and when mining starts or stops, exactly this: its random install ID, its version, whether it is mining, and whether it uses the CPU or GPU — no wallet address, no name, no hardware details. You can turn it off in Settings, under “Count me in the public miner counter”.

    In MINTRO 0.4.1 no counter service is set up, so nothing is sent.

  7. 07Cookies

    Cookies

    This website uses no cookies and no tracking pixels.

  8. 08No sale

    We do not sell your data

    We don't sell, rent or trade personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

  9. 09Contact

    Changes and contact

    We'll update this page if what MINTRO collects changes; the date at the top shows the last change. Questions about your data can be sent to hello@aiteleresearch.com.
