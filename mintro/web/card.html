A real payout is the final proof: once a coin passes the pool's minimum, its coins should arrive in your wallet within the pool's payout interval.

What to expect
  • Small amounts. A laptop mines cents' worth a day; a desktop a little more. MINTRO shows earnings after electricity so you can judge for yourself.
  • A lottery, not an income. Most new coins never gain value. The upside is holding a slice of a coin early, in case it does.
  • Your coins, your wallets. Pools pay straight to your addresses. MINTRO never holds your coins and takes no fee.
  • Windows Security will flag the miner. It treats every crypto miner as a threat; the card shows how to allow MINTRO's miner folder.
  • Updates install themselves. Each one is checked against the publisher's signature and installs while you're away from the PC. Mining keeps going.
  • Counted, anonymously. MINTRO tells the public miner counter that one PC is mining: a random ID, nothing else. Settings has the off switch.
